Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about lead times, customization, bridal keepsakes, and nationwide deliveries.

schedule

How long does an order typically take?

For Lahore deliveries, ready keepsake boxes and standard bouquets take 24 to 48 hours. Completely bespoke wedding platters, bridal trousseau sets, and large bouquets require 3 to 5 working days. Express rush orders are accommodated upon studio confirmation.

palette

Can I change colors or match my bridal dress?

Yes, absolutely! Every keepsake is 100% handcrafted to order. You can send us photos of your Nikkah, Barat, or Walima bridal dress, or fabric swatches on WhatsApp, and our crafters will match shades, ribbons, and velvet tones seamlessly.

payments

What payment options are accepted?

We accept JazzCash, Raast, SadaPay, NayaPay, and online Direct Bank Transfers (Meezan, HBL, Alfalah). We require a 50% advance deposit to secure your crafting date and purchase bespoke materials, with the remainder payable upon final photo approval before dispatch.

local_shipping

Do you deliver nationwide outside Lahore?

Yes, we ship nationwide to Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Sialkot, Peshawar, Quetta, and all serviced postal zones across Pakistan via express courier with full tracking updates.

inventory_2

How are delicate items protected in transit?

Every keepsake is safely encased in multilayered bubble wrap, custom shock-absorbent thermocol framing, and rigid double-wall corrugated corrugated boxes labeled "Fragile / Handle With Care" to guarantee 100% safe arrival at your doorstep.

edit

Can I include a handwritten calligraphy card?

Complimentary! Every single order includes a luxurious 350 GSM cotton card with handwritten Nastaliq Urdu or romantic English script, finished with an authentic gold wax seal bearing our studio emblem.
