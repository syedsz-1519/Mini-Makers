Atelier Craftsmanship

Handcrafted Anatomy & Details

Everything you need to know about our signature ribbon sculpting, materials, and care.

Every single bouquet is assembled as a museum-worthy floral trousseau. Included in every order:

check_circle 12 to 18 Handcrafted Roses: Individually creased double-faced polyester satin ribbons.
check_circle Integrated Fairy Micro-lights: Warm champagne glow with replaceable discrete battery pack.
check_circle Korean Matte Waterproof Wrapping: Multi-tiered pastel blush and frosted parchment pleating.
check_circle Calligraphed Note Card: Heavy 350 GSM cotton paper with gold foil or Urdu Nastaliq ink.

We know how delicate handcrafted floral arrangements are. Every bouquet is anchored inside a custom rigid double-walled shipping container with interior foam support so petals never bend or flatten during transport.

  • Lahore & Pattoki: Same-day or 24-hour hand delivery available via dedicated studio couriers.
  • Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad: 2–3 business days via premier tracked air courier services.
  • Fragile Sealed Guarantee: If your bouquet encounters any transit distress, our atelier will repair or replace it immediately.

Each bouquet requires between 40 to 60 meters of hand-selected satin ribbon and 6 dedicated hours of precision folding and hot-sealing by our women artisans.

Everlasting Care Instructions:

Keep away from direct prolonged harsh sunlight to avoid textile fading. Use a soft camera lens duster or hairdryer on cold setting to gently clear surface dust over the years. Never immerse in water.
