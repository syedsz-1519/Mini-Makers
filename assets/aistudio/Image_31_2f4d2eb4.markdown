## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the warmth, emotional tenderness, and bespoke refinement of a boutique personalized gift atelier. It balances the nostalgia of handcrafted memorabilia with a polished, editorial presentation reminiscent of luxury bridal and celebratory stationery. 

The aesthetic is romantic, editorial, and tactile:
- **Atmospheric Palette**: Layers of warm cream, delicate blush, and deep plum anchoring velvety dusty rose and brushed champagne gold.
- **Architectural Framing**: Romanesque arched card shapes, curved gallery viewports, and pill-formed interaction points evoking traditional paper-cut stationery and hand-bound keepsake boxes.
- **Micro-Decorations**: Subtle sparkling accents, gilded hairline borders, and soft organic ribbon motifs that frame bespoke customer journeys without cluttering transactional focus.
- **Emotional Intent**: Eliciting tenderness, heirloom value, and effortless trust for high-touch, direct-to-artisan communication.

## Colors
The color hierarchy creates a soft, tactile sanctuary. Warm surfaces prevent sterile white space, while high-contrast plum text maintains effortless legibility.

- **Primary (`#B5446E`)**: A dusty mauve rose applied to primary interactive elements, critical actions, active tabs, and emphasized editorial italics.
- **Secondary (`#F7C6D3`)**: A velvety pastel pink utilized for soft surface tints, pill badge backdrops, and active toggle containers.
- **Tertiary (`#C9A36B`)**: A muted artisanal gold used for hairline borders, premium embellishments, rating stars, and badge trims.
- **Neutral Surface & Canvas**: `#FDF6F0` (warm cream) forms the main background wash, accented by `#FBE4EA` (warm blush) for inset panels, side sheets, and layered arches.
- **Typography & Structural Neutrals**: Primary copy is rendered in `#4A2038` (deep plum), replacing harsh black with an ink-like richness. Secondary and muted text uses `#6D435A` (warm muted plum).
- **Functional Semantic**: Validation uses soft rose-red for errors (`#C23B58`), warm amber for notes (`#D98E32`), and soft sage for success (`#5B8E6F`).

## Typography
The typographic system pairs the literary grace of **Playfair Display** with the understated, crisp geometry of **DM Sans**.

- **Editorial Headlines**: Reserve Playfair Display for storytelling, section titles, and product titles. Utilize standard medium or semibold weights, frequently accenting single keywords with italics to provide an intimate handcrafted signature.
- **Body & Captions**: DM Sans provides high-clarity contrast against expressive headers. Generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensure readability against blush and cream backgrounds.
- **Micro-Labels**: Category tags, collection markers, and input labels utilize `label-caps` (all-caps DM Sans with wide `0.12em` letter spacing) to instill a curated, boutique catalog character.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an asymmetrical, editorial rhythm that echoes high-end print lookbooks rather than standard e-commerce grids.

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with 40px (`margin-lg`) canvas padding and 40px (`gutter-lg`) item separation. Alternating arched hero showcases and offset feature cards.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 40px margins and 28px gutters.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 20px margins and 20px gutters. Horizontal scrolling carousels with peak cards preserve an expansive feel.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace**: Generous section separation (`space-xl` scaled to 5rem+ on desktop) allows handcrafted elements breathing room, reinforcing exclusivity and deliberate curation.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through ambient tinted glows, soft parchment layers, and delicate metallic hairline trims instead of aggressive drop shadows.

- **Ambient Dream Tints**: Shadows utilize the primary pigment diluted to extreme softness:
  - *Resting Card Elevation*: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(181, 68, 110, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(74, 32, 56, 0.03)`
  - *Hover Elevation*: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(181, 68, 110, 0.14), 0 4px 12px rgba(74, 32, 56, 0.04)`
  - *Floating Quick Actions (WhatsApp)*: `0 12px 32px rgba(181, 68, 110, 0.22), 0 2px 8px rgba(74, 32, 56, 0.06)`
- **Layering & Borders**: Surfaces stack cream (`#FDF6F0`) over blush (`#FBE4EA`) with crisp 1px borders colored in `rgba(181, 68, 110, 0.12)` or soft gold `rgba(201, 163, 107, 0.28)`.
- **Glass Insets**: Overlay elements and navigation banners use frosted panels: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(253, 246, 240, 0.85)`.

## Shapes
Shapes emphasize rounded, flowing silhouettes that evoke envelopes, ribbons, and classical European arches:

- **Arch Frames**: Showcase imagery and hero showcases utilize a top-arch border radius (`rounded-t-full` or `rounded-t-[120px] rounded-b-[24px]`).
- **Pill System**: Buttons, badges, search bars, and floating triggers feature fully circular cap ends (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Content Cards**: Internal cards and chat-bubble modules use extra-large radii (`rounded-2xl` / 24px to 28px) with organic balance.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Pill Button**: Background is a gradient from `#B5446E` to `#A23B61`. Text is white, font is `DM Sans` (semibold), with `padding: 0.875rem 2rem`. Subtle gold outer ring on focus/hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #FDF6F0, 0 0 0 4px #C9A36B`).
- **Secondary Boutique Button**: Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in `#C9A36B`, text in `#4A2038`. Soft blush hover fill (`#FBE4EA`).
- **Floating WhatsApp Quick-Action**: Fixed floating action button at the bottom-right viewport (56px × 56px circle or expanded pill on scroll). Styled with rich emerald-gold or velvety mauve casing with the classic WhatsApp icon, framed by an ambient glow (`rgba(181, 68, 110, 0.2)`).

### Product Cards & Showcase Arches
- **Arch Portfolio Card**: Visual assets sit inside a portrait container with a top arch (`border-radius: 140px 140px 24px 24px`). Encased in a 1px border of `rgba(201, 163, 107, 0.35)`.
- **Content Footers**: Placed directly beneath the arch, containing an uppercase micro-category, a Playfair Display product title, price in deep plum, and a pill-shaped "Inquire" button.

### Chips, Pills & Filters
- **Category Filter Chips**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`). Unselected state: warm blush background (`#FBE4EA`), `#6D435A` text. Selected state: dusty rose (`#B5446E`) with white text and an inner 1px champagne highlight.

### Chat & Testimonial Cards
- **WhatsApp-Inspired Testimonial Bubbles**: Styled as candid dialogue excerpts with rounded asymmetrical corners (`rounded-2xl rounded-tl-sm`), light blush-tinted container (`#FFFFFF` with `#FBE4EA` tint), timestamp in `#6D435A`, small gilded double-check icon, and customer name displayed in `label-caps`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Bespoke Inquiry Inputs**: Generous pill fields (`rounded-full`) or soft-rounded blocks (`rounded-xl`). Background `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border in `rgba(181, 68, 110, 0.2)`. On focus, transitions smoothly to a 1.5px `#C9A36B` border with a subtle mauve-tinted glow.

### Decorative Separators
- **Ribbon & Sparkle Dividers**: Thin 1px gradient line fading from transparent to `#C9A36B` to transparent, interrupted at center by a four-point vector star sparkle or an organic hand-tied ribbon knot silhouette.
