Catalogue & Order Questions

Because Mini Makers does not mass manufacture anything. Every wedding platter, bouquet stem count, and personalized pen requires your specific name spellings, Urdu preferences, and color palette. Chatting directly on WhatsApp guarantees you get exactly what you envision without sizing errors.
We recommend ordering 5 to 7 days before your Baraat, Nikkah, or Mehndi event. However, for urgent rush orders in Lahore & Pattoki, express crafting within 48 hours is possible depending on current workshop queue.
We accept Bank Transfer (Meezan Bank / HBL / Alfalah), JazzCash, and EasyPaisa. Because each order is custom-carved or personalized, a 50% advance deposit confirms your crafting slot, with the balance upon video approval or COD where applicable.
